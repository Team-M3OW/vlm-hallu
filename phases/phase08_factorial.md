# Phase 8 — Factorial over the zoom format

## 1. Research question
Phase 7's intervention bundled several things (extra image, connector text, higher resolution).
Which factor is doing the work?

## 2. Finding and contribution (plain English)
It set up the decomposition that Phase 9/10 completed: the recovery is much larger when the crop is
presented **alone** than when it sits beside the full scene. That gap became the Phase 10 finding.

## 3. Numbers that changed
Established the contrast later quantified as **crop alone 40.4% vs [full + crop] 12.8%**.

## 4. Keep in paper: 2/10
Subsumed by Phase 10's cleaner factorial. Cite Phase 10 instead.

## 5. Experiment, step by step
1. Enumerate the factors bundled in Phase 7: image count, connector text, ordering, resolution.
2. Build arms varying them.
3. Identify which contrast carries the effect; hand off to Phase 10 for the controlled version.
