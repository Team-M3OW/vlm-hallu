# Phase 26 — Is the failure in routing or in use?

## 1. Research question
Matching on the model's *answer* rather than on the item, is the deficit a failure to route attention
to the evidence, or a failure to use evidence that was routed?

## 2. Finding and contribution (plain English)
A **use** failure, not a routing failure. This agrees with Phase 24's controls and points the same
way everything else eventually does: the information at the attended location is insufficient.

## 3. Numbers that changed
Answer-matched contrast supports "C2 is a use failure". (Superseded in precision by §13, which shows
directly that nothing is encoded to use.)

## 4. Keep in paper: 4/10
Subsumed by §13, which makes the same claim with a much better instrument. Cite as converging
evidence in one sentence.

## 5. Experiment, step by step
1. Pair items by the model's emitted answer rather than by item identity.
2. Compare attention routing and downstream use within matched pairs.
3. Attribute the residual to routing or to use.
